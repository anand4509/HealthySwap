ABOUT FOODWISE

Make Smarter Food Choices

FoodWise is an educational food analysis project designed to help users understand differences between healthy and highly processed foods.

The project combines food information, nutrition comparison, personalized general suggestions and healthier food swaps.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Nutrition values and recommendations shown by this educational project are estimates and should not be treated as medical or clinical dietary advice.
